How it works

Community knowledge, carefully shared

Every accessibility report on AccessAble is contributed by people with lived experience. We surface honest, nuanced information rather than oversimplified ratings.

1

Discover

Search for any public place and see a detailed, feature-by-feature accessibility breakdown based on real community reports.

2

Report

Submit what you experienced — which features were available, which weren't, and anything that other visitors should know.

3

Improve

Each new report makes the picture clearer for everyone. Conflicting reports are flagged, not hidden — because nuance matters.

Community

Your visit. Your knowledge. Help others plan theirs.

Every report — no matter how small — helps someone plan a journey with confidence.
